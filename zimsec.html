LEVEL 1 - ECD

ECD A & ECD B - (Cambridge Early Years 1-3)

Age: 3-5 Years | ECD A=3-4yrs, ECD B=4-5yrs
Learning Areas: Language Arts | Mathematics & Science | Heritage - Social Science | Visual & Performing Arts | Physical Education | ICT
LEVEL 2 - PRIMARY

Grade 1 to Grade 7 - (Cambridge Primary Stage 1-6)

Age: 5-13 Years | Grade 7 ZIMSEC Exam
Core: English | Mathematics | Science & Technology | Agriculture | Heritage - Social Studies | FAREME | ICT | Visual & Performing Arts | PE
LEVEL 3 - JUNIOR SECONDARY

Form 1 & Form 2 (ZJC Level) - (Cambridge Lower Secondary Stage 7-8)

Age: 13-15 Years
Core: English | Mathematics | Combined Science | Heritage | Agriculture
Electives: History | Geography | Commerce | Accounts | Computer Science | Food & Nutrition | Fashion | Woodwork | Metalwork | Building | Art
LEVEL 4 - SENIOR SECONDARY

Form 3 & Form 4 O-Level - (Cambridge IGCSE Stage 10-11)

Age: 15-17 Years | ZIMSEC O-Level
Compulsory: English | Mathematics | Science (Combined / Biology, Chemistry, Physics)
Commercials & Arts: Commerce | Accounts | Business Studies | Economics | Geography | History | Literature | Heritage
Practicals: Computer Science | Agriculture | Food | Fashion | Wood | Metal | Building | Art | Music
LEVEL 5 - ADVANCED

Form 5 & Form 6 A-Level - (Cambridge AS & A-Level)

Age: 17-19 Years | University Entry
Sciences: Biology | Chemistry | Physics | Mathematics | Further Mathematics | Computer Science | Agriculture | Geography
Commercials: Accounting | Business Studies | Economics | Management of Business
Arts: History | Literature | Divinity | Shona/Ndebele | Sociology | Art | Heritage
