STEP 1 · READ AND SIGN

School Rules & Family Agreement

Please read these expectations with the learner. The application form opens after an authorised parent / guardian, or an adult learner, signs below.

  1. Attendance and punctuality: learners are expected to attend all scheduled classes, arrive on time and follow the school’s absence reporting procedure. Families should inform the school promptly when a learner is ill or absent.
  2. Respect and behaviour: every learner must treat students, staff and visitors with respect. Bullying, harassment, discrimination, threats, fighting and disruptive conduct are not acceptable. Concerns should be reported to school authorities.
  3. Uniform and presentation: learners must follow the current uniform and personal presentation requirements communicated by the school, including any approved exceptions.
  4. Learning and honesty: learners are expected to participate, complete work on time and submit their own work. Cheating, plagiarism, impersonation, tampering with marks or disrupting assessments may lead to disciplinary review.
  5. Safety and safeguarding: learners must follow staff instructions, stay in authorised areas and report hazards or safety concerns. Weapons, alcohol, illegal drugs and other dangerous items are prohibited on school premises and at school activities.
  6. Devices and online conduct: personal devices may be used only when permitted. Learners must protect passwords and personal information, use school platforms appropriately, and must not record, copy or share another person’s information or images without permission.
  7. School property: books, equipment, facilities and other property must be used responsibly. Loss or damage should be reported promptly; families may be contacted about avoidable damage in line with school policy.
  8. Health and emergency information: families must provide accurate health, allergy and emergency-contact information and update the school when it changes. Medication must be disclosed and handled through the school’s approved process.
  9. Collection and transport: families must keep authorised collection details current and follow the school’s arrival, dismissal and transport arrangements. Learners may not leave campus during school hours without permission.
  10. Family communication: families should keep contact details current, read official school notices and respond to requests for meetings or information. Messages should use the school’s monitored communication channels where available.
  11. Fees and school notices: families are responsible for reviewing the current fee schedule and payment dates issued by the school. Applicable prices are maintained by school management and may be updated through official notice; this agreement does not set a fee amount.
  12. School decisions: rule concerns are reviewed by the appropriate school authority. The school will communicate with the learner and family and apply its current discipline and safeguarding procedures.
  13. Registration and information: information supplied must be accurate. An application remains subject to Admissions review; signing this agreement does not itself confirm enrolment or activate a portal account.
Use the white box to sign. Clear and sign again if needed.
