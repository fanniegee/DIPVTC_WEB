School Rules & Family Agreement

Please read these expectations with the learner. The application form opens after an authorised parent / guardian, or an adult learner, signs below.

  1. Attendance and punctuality: learners are expected to attend scheduled classes, arrive on time and follow the absence-reporting procedure. Families should notify the school promptly when a learner is ill or absent.
  2. Respect, bullying and discipline: learners must treat students, staff and visitors respectfully. Bullying, harassment, discrimination, threats and fighting are prohibited. Reports are reviewed by the school’s discipline committee; appropriate age-sensitive, non-violent consequences may include restorative learning or suspension after a fair review.
  3. No corporal punishment: no teacher, staff member, parent or other person may beat or physically punish a learner at school, in a hostel or during a school activity. Use the school’s approved non-violent safeguarding and discipline process.
  4. Uniform: only the approved school uniform may be worn on school premises unless an authorised school official approves an exception. The current uniform-violation penalty is per incident.
  5. Grooming and personal presentation: learners should maintain tidy, safe and hygienic grooming in line with the school’s communicated standards. The school will consider cultural, religious and medical needs and apply any requirements fairly.
  6. Learning and honesty: learners are expected to participate, complete work on time and submit their own work. Cheating, plagiarism, impersonation, tampering with marks or disrupting assessments may lead to disciplinary review.
  7. Language at school: English is the school’s normal language for instruction and official school communication where applicable. Families may use their home languages at home.
  8. Safety and safeguarding: learners must follow staff instructions, remain in authorised areas and report hazards or safety concerns. Weapons, alcohol, illegal drugs and other dangerous items are prohibited on school premises and at school activities.
  9. Devices and online conduct: personal devices may be used only when permitted. Learners must protect passwords and personal information, use school platforms appropriately, and must not record, copy or share another person’s information or images without permission.
  10. Health and emergency information: families must provide accurate health, allergy and emergency-contact information and update the school when it changes. Medication must be disclosed and handled through the school’s approved process.
  11. Collection and transport: families must keep authorised collection details current and follow the school’s arrival, dismissal and transport arrangements. Learners may not leave campus during school hours without permission.
  12. School property: books, equipment, facilities and other property must be used responsibly. Damage should be reported promptly; after review, families may be asked to cover reasonable repair or replacement costs in line with school policy.
  13. Fees and term payments: school fees cover a -month term. The current Primary full-term amount is or monthly instalments from ; High School is or monthly instalments from . The applicable term fee remains payable even when a learner is absent for part of the term.
  14. Boarding charges: the current primary boarding charge is per month; the current high-school boarding charge is per month. Confirm the learner’s applicable charges with Admissions.
  15. Payment dates and late interest: monthly payments are expected by the of each month and must be paid no later than the . Late payments may incur interest at the current rate of per month, as shown in the school’s approved fee schedule.
  16. Registration charges: annual registration at the start of each school year is currently for day learners and for boarding learners. Each new registration also includes a one-time report book charge of .
  17. Term toiletries: each learner must bring the school’s requested toiletries once per term: Harpic, Sunlight liquid and nine rolls of two-ply toilet paper. Admissions will advise families if the school updates this list.
  18. Respectful family and staff communication: parents, learners and staff must communicate respectfully. Complaints, concerns and requests for discussion should be raised through the school’s designated channels and referred to the appropriate committee when needed. Staff conduct is handled under the school’s staff procedures.
  19. School decisions: the designated school committee oversees discipline and reviews complaints and negotiations from families or staff. The school will communicate with the learner and family and apply current safeguarding and discipline procedures.
  20. School property, family communication and records: families should keep contact details current, read official notices and respond to reasonable requests for information or meetings. Learner and family information supplied on the application must be accurate. The application remains subject to Admissions review; signing does not itself confirm enrolment or activate a portal account.
