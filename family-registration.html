Student Registration

Students can apply directly. A parent or guardian may help complete the form. Admissions reviews each application before the student portal is opened.

How registration works: Create the student account with the student’s own working email and a password they can remember. The account remains locked while Admissions reviews the application. Once approved, the student signs in with the same email and password. A parent or guardian can help fill in the form; do not use the parent’s email as the student email.

At least 10 characters. The student will use this after approval.
