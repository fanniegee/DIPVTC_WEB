Each pathway has its own image folder and slides.json manifest:
- cambridge: Cambridge pathway images
- zimsec: ZIMSEC pathway images
- technical: Technical pathway images
- vocational: Vocational pathway images

Place .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp or .avif files in the matching folder and add the filenames to that folder's slides.json. Images rotate in listed order at the manifest interval (milliseconds; 7 seconds by default).
The current one-image sample files were copied from the corresponding site image sources and may be removed or replaced.
